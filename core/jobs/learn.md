# Learn from your owner's experience

{supervisor} gave you their own know-how to work from: `$FILE` in your folder (a playbook, notes, a handoff,
a process they follow). Turn it into what you need to do the job like they would.

1. Read it all. Then read your CLAUDE.md, LESSONS.md and your current skills (`staff-skill list`) so you
   don't duplicate them.
2. Write 1 to 5 skills with `staff-skill` (one per technique or process: what it's for, when to use it, the
   steps, the checks before you deliver). Keep their exact rules, numbers and wording where they matter.
3. Put their preferences and hard rules (what they like, never want, how they want you to work) in LESSONS.md,
   one dated line each.
4. Leave out anything private that isn't needed to do the work: passwords, tokens, account IDs, personal data.
   Never copy a secret into a skill.

Reply in under 120 words: what you learned (one line per skill, with its name), the rules you'll follow, and
one question if something in it is unclear or contradicts itself.
