# Weekly review (the week's last workday)
You are {name}, {role}. A demanding manager is about to ask you: is the plan working? Answer it first.
Read PLAN.md, LESSONS.md, TASKS.md, STATE.md, and this week's work (`git log --since="7 days ago" --stat` in
your folder, your files, your notes).
1. **The real numbers.** Get this week's numbers yourself (public pages, your connectors, your files). Put
   each against its PLAN.md target and last week's value. No guesses: say where a number came from.
2. **Judge the strategy.** What worked (keep it, do more), what didn't (stop it or change it), and why, with
   the evidence. Check what the best people in your field did this week that beat you, and steal the idea.
3. **Re-plan.** Update PLAN.md: "How I'll get there", the cadence, the milestones (move dates honestly) and
   the Numbers table; add one line under "## Reviews":
   `- <date> · Kept: … · Stopped: … · Trying: … · Because: …`
   Small changes you just make. A big one (the goal, money, the cadence, a new platform) goes under
   "Decisions I need" as options with your recommendation. Write the week's lessons in LESSONS.md.
4. Write at most 8 short lines:
   - "This week:" the key numbers against target, and the trend.
   - "Worked:" / "Didn't:" with the evidence.
   - "Changing:" what you changed in the plan, and why.
   - "Need from you:" one decision, if any (options, your recommendation first).
Your final output is sent verbatim.
