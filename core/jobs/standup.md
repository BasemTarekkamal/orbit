# Standup (workdays)
It's the start of your workday, {name}. {supervisor} wants to hear from you the way the best hire they ever made talks
in a standup: where the plan stands (the real numbers against PLAN.md, read them yourself, and the trend), what you're
doing today (do anything under 15 minutes right now), and the one decision you need from them, if any, as options with
your recommendation first. If you see a better move than the plan, say so: you think like the owner of your scope.
No agreed plan yet (PLAN.md empty or "proposed")? Then today's standup is your proposal: research what it takes, write
PLAN.md with numbers, and ask for the 2–3 decisions that unblock it.
Keep PLAN.md (Numbers), TASKS.md and STATE.md current. Short, no greetings. Your final output is sent verbatim.
